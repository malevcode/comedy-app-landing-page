PRIVACY POLICY

EFFECTIVE DATE: APRIL 7, 2026

Comediq is a personal performance tool for stand-up comedians. We take your material seriously — your jokes are your IP, and your data is yours. This policy explains exactly what we collect, why, and who touches it.

Who We Are

Comediq ("we," "us," or "our") is operated by Adam Malev. You can reach us at adam@comediq.us or via the support page.

What We Collect

When you use the Comediq app, the following data is generated and stored:

  • Audio recordings — the audio file of your set, uploaded to secure cloud storage
  • Transcripts — word-level transcripts with timestamps, generated from your audio
  • Performance metadata — venue, date, set duration, score, laugh timestamps, pause data
  • Joke data — bit names, punchlines, setups, feedback, performance history
  • Confidence ratings — self-reported ratings you log manually
  • Quick notes — freeform text notes you type in the app
  • Set plans — the run orders you build before shows
  • Post-set reflections — audience reception, confidence score, personal notes you log after a set

We do not collect your name, email address, or any account information. Comediq is currently a single-user app with no authentication system.

Third-Party Services That Process Your Data

Processing your sets requires sending data to these third-party services:

AssemblyAI (assemblyai.com) — Your audio is uploaded to AssemblyAI for speech-to-text transcription. AssemblyAI processes audio on servers in the United States. Their privacy policy is at assemblyai.com/privacy.

Anthropic / Claude API (anthropic.com) — Your transcript is sent to Anthropic's Claude API for analysis. Claude identifies bits, scores your set, and detects themes. Anthropic processes data on servers in the United States. Their privacy policy is at anthropic.com/privacy.

Supabase (supabase.com) — All structured data (sets, bits, identities, notes, plans) and audio files are stored in Supabase. Supabase is a cloud database platform. Their privacy policy is at supabase.com/privacy.

How We Use Your Data

  • To transcribe and analyze your comedy sets
  • To track your joke performance over time
  • To surface which material is working and which isn't
  • To let you build and manage your active set list

We do not use your data for advertising. We do not sell your data. We do not train AI models on your content.

Data Retention

Your data is retained indefinitely until you delete it. You can delete individual sets from within the app. There is no account deletion flow currently — if you want all your data removed, contact us at adam@comediq.us and we will delete it manually within 7 days.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access all data stored about you
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Export your data (contact us)

To exercise any of these rights, email adam@comediq.us.

Security

Data is stored in Supabase using service-role credentials. Audio files are stored in private Supabase Storage buckets. The API is served over HTTPS via Railway. We do not implement end-to-end encryption at the application layer — treat this as a personal productivity tool, not a secure vault for sensitive material.

Children

Comediq is intended for adults (18+). We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.

Changes to This Policy

If we materially change how we handle your data, we will update this page and change the effective date. For significant changes, we may notify you in the app.
